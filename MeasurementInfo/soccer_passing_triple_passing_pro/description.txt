Begin from the marked orange "starting line". Perform a sprint through the starting gate and pass the ball three times to the rebound wall. Select the side to start and pass the ball directing it toward the rebound wall and receive the ball on same side of the middle cone. Now, move the ball to the other side of the cone and pass the ball directing it toward the rebound wall and receive the ball on same side of the middle cone. Move again to the other side, pass to the rebound and receive the ball same side. After the final reception of the ball then proceed through the finish gate.
