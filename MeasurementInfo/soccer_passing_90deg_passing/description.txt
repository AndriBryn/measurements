Begin from the marked orange "starting line". Perform a sprint through the starting gate and pass the ball four times to the rebound wall. Start passing the ball between markers A and B, directing it toward the rebound wall. As you receive the ball, make a 90-degree turn to your right. Now, pass the ball between markers B and C, aiming for the rebound wall. As you regain possession of the ball, execute a 90-degree turn to your left. Repeat these steps to complete four cycles of passing and receiving. After the final reception of the ball then proceed through the finish gate.
